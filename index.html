Chapter 06 — The Organisers

Who is Focus?

Assalamu alaikkum wa rahmatullahi wa barakhathahu

Focus is a group of professionals, for professionals — with the intent to present Islam in its pure form, and to give the professional community an opportunity to understand Islam from its basic sources: the Quran and Sunnah. We focus on creating awareness among the professional community about our ultimate purpose of life — what it is, and why.

Our Vision

To accomplish the creed, the rituals and the actions in the way of the examples set by the “Salaf-us-Salih” — the virtuous predecessors of Prophet Muhammad (SA) — based on the Quran and Sunnah.

Our Mission

  • Invite people to the Quran and Sunnah as understood by the Salaf-as-Salih, and propagate the examples of the pious predecessors’ way of life among Muslims.
  • Make the believers wise enough to live a life based on Monotheism (Thouheed).
  • Provide awareness about “Shirk” and “Bidha’a” (polytheism and innovations in Islam).
  • Inculcate moral and ethical values in individuals.
  • Cultivate Islamic values among professionals.
  • Strive to reach the true message of Islam to people of all faiths.
